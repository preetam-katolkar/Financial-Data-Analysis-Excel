# Financial Data Analysis & Sales Forecasting in Excel

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project built in **Microsoft Excel** using the *Financial Sample* dataset (700 records). This project covers Data Cleaning, Interactive Dashboarding, Diagnostic Analysis, Time-Series Forecasting, and Prescriptive Business Recommendations.

## Key Findings
* **Top Performing Segments:** **Government** ($52.5M Sales) and **Small Business** generate the highest revenue and profit across all regions.
* **Loss-Making Segment:** **Enterprise** is the only loss-making category with a net loss of **-$614,545.63**.
* **Correlation Analysis:** `Discounts` vs. `Profit` shows a positive correlation of **+0.38**, indicating that discounts drive volume and are not the primary cause of Enterprise losses.
* **6-Month Forecast (Jan–Jun 2015):** Projected monthly sales range between **$157,611 and $180,795**, with peak demand expected in **January** and **April 2015**.

## Prescriptive Recommendations
* **Scale High-Margin Segments:** Allocate additional marketing budget and inventory to **Government** and **Small Business** segments.
* **Restructure Enterprise Pricing:** Re-evaluate discount bands and production costs (COGS) in the **Enterprise** segment to turn losses into positive margins.
* **Inventory Readiness:** Increase stock levels ahead of **January** and **April 2015** to capture projected peak demand.
